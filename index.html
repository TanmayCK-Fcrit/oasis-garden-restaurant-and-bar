THE OASIS EXPERIENCE

A Lush Escape on Pune-Solapur Road

Escape city congestion into a tranquil expanse of landscaped greenery. Oasis brings together three complete atmospheres: expansive open-air lawns for evening gatherings, an intimate AC banquet hall for private events, and a vibrant garden bar serving signature cocktails, single malts, and chilled beers.

From charcoal-fired tandoor platters and rich Mughlai gravies to coastal Mangalorean seafood and Chinese bowls, our kitchen prepares every dish fresh using purified water and refined cooking standards.
